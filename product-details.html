At TEOCO, we believe in simplifying complex business problems and workflows.

BillTrak SmartMatch
Designed for users of BillTrak, TEOCO’s flagship invoice management system. This module helps CSPs eliminate costly errors and increase data reliability. Leveraging machine learning and geo-coding, SmartMatch normalizes wholesale circuit data to improve and enhance the matching of invoiced circuits to circuit inventory.
Contract Management
Gain easy access to contract data, vendor rate sheets, and more for efficient tracking and comparison of vendor contract terms and conditions. Seamlessly integrates with other SmartCircuit modules to detect, track, and claim credits for SLA violations.
Circuit Inventory
Maintain accurate insights into every wholesale circuit throughout its lifecycle – from contract to revenue – and visualize it all with just a click. Enables users to quickly generate map-based views of the telecom network for easy analysis.
